Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

Pelliper is an open-source, local-first desktop email client. Your email data, attachments, and credentials stay on your own device and are never sent to any Pelliper developer servers.

1. Data Accessed

When you connect an email account (such as a Google/Gmail account via OAuth 2.0), Pelliper requests permission to access your mailbox (https://mail.google.com/) solely to synchronize folders, read messages and attachments, manage message flags/folders, and send emails on your behalf via IMAP and SMTP.

2. How Data Is Stored & Used

  • Local Storage Only: Synchronized email headers, message bodies, and downloaded attachments are stored locally in an SQLite database and cache directory on your computer.
  • Credentials: OAuth 2.0 access/refresh tokens and passwords are stored locally in your operating system's secret service/keyring (org.freedesktop.secrets).
  • Direct Connection: Pelliper connects directly from your device to your email provider's IMAP, SMTP, and OAuth servers. No intermediate proxy or developer backend is used.

3. Data Sharing & Telemetry

Pelliper does not collect analytics, telemetry, crash reports, or usage tracking, and does not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third parties.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

Pelliper's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You can remove an account from Pelliper at any time, delete Pelliper's local cache/database files on your device, and revoke Pelliper's OAuth access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

6. Contact

For privacy questions or support, please open an issue on the Pelliper GitHub repository.
